# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Portfolio visitor and the author at home. Context: Mac idle or lock screen in a dim room (evening desk, bedroom, Wembley Park flat). They are not managing flights; they glance and look away. Success is quiet recognition that something is overhead, not dashboard density.

## Product Purpose

Overhead Flights is a minimal ambient display: show the single nearest aircraft above you as screensaver content. Replace decorative scenery with one living fact. No maps, no lists, no chrome. Empty sky stays pure black.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, airy, silver-on-night. Editorial restraint over tech dashboard. Like a museum wall label for the sky: calm, sparse, slightly precious without luxury cliché.

## Anti-references

- FlightRadar-style maps, trails, and multi-aircraft clutter
- Neon aviation / ATC terminal green-on-black
- SaaS metric cards, big-number dashboards, glassmorphism widgets
- Loud lock-screen widgets that fight the system clock
- Pure pure-white (#fff) screaming type on pure black without hierarchy

## Design Principles

1. **One plane, one breath** — only the nearest aircraft; no competing hierarchy.
2. **Coexist with the clock** — type must live under macOS lock chrome without shouting.
3. **Silence is a feature** — no aircraft means no UI; errors never surface.
4. **Hierarchy by air, not by bold** — scale, weight, and opacity; not color accents.
5. **OLED-honest night** — near-black field, soft silver type, no decorative glow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Prefer high enough contrast on callsign for glanceability; secondary lines may be quieter.
- Respect reduced motion (no obligatory drift/entrance when system prefers reduced motion).
- No information only in color; English labels for bearing (existing).
